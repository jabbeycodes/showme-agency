How we work

A clear path from first conversation to ongoing service

  1. Consultation

    A conversation about your goals, current setup and constraints. No obligation and no sales script.

  2. Assessment

    We review your systems, accounts, devices, workflows and marketing, depending on what you asked us to look at.

  3. Roadmap

    A prioritised list of recommendations, separated into quick wins, projects and ongoing services.

  4. Proposal & agreement

    A written proposal with scope, deliverables, exclusions, timeline and price. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  5. Onboarding

    We document your current setup, agree access and contacts, fix urgent issues and set up reporting.
