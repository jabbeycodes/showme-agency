Payment terms & methods

Simple, upfront terms.

  • Projects: 50% deposit to start, 50% on launch. The final payment is only due after you approve a working preview.
  • Retainers & monthly plans: billed monthly in advance. The Growth retainer has a 3-month minimum.
  • Ad spend: separate, prepaid in cedis and paid directly to the ad platforms (recommended from GHS 1,500/month).
  • Diaspora clients: USD quotes for clients in the US, UK and Canada, payable by card or international bank transfer.
  • Quotes are valid for 30 days and list any third-party costs (licences, ad spend, payment fees) separately.

Ways to pay

  • MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and AT Money
  • Bank transfer (GHS)
  • Debit or credit card via a secure payment link
