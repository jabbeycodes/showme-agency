Pricing

How pricing works.

Every engagement is quoted in writing for your agreed scope. No surprises, and nothing starts until you approve the proposal.

Projects

A fixed quote for a defined outcome, such as a website, a migration, an automation or an application.

  • Fixed price for the agreed scope
  • Changes quoted before any extra work
  • 50% to start, 50% on launch after preview approval

Managed monthly services

A predictable monthly fee for ongoing IT support and administration, marketing or application maintenance.

  • Clear inclusions and exclusions
  • Billed monthly in advance
  • Any minimum term stated in your proposal

Pricing basis for managed IT [TO CONFIRM: per user, per device or per package]

Advisory

Fixed-fee consultations, assessments, workshops and technology roadmaps.

  • Fee agreed up front
  • Written recommendations you keep
  • No obligation to buy implementation

Payment

  • Debit or credit card via a secure payment link
  • Bank transfer
  • Invoices in US dollars (USD)

You approve a working preview before the final 50% is due. If it does not match the agreed scope, we fix it first.

Frequently asked questions

Why are prices not listed?

Because scope drives cost. A five-person office and a fifty-person organisation need different things, so we quote a fixed price for your agreed scope after a short assessment.

Which currency do you quote in?

Yes. Quotes are in US dollars (USD), and the exact figure is fixed in your proposal.

How can I pay?

Debit or credit card via a secure payment link, or bank transfer.

Are there hidden costs?

No. Third-party costs such as licences, hardware, ad spend or hosting are listed in your proposal and passed through or paid by you directly.

Request pricing for your scope.

Tell us what you need. We reply within one business day with next steps and, after a short assessment, a fixed written quote.
