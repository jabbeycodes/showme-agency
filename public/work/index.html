Solution Restaurants & caterers

Restaurant ordering system

The problem it solves: Restaurants that take most orders on WhatsApp often lose time at rush hour copying orders onto paper and checking MoMo screenshots by hand.

Key features

  • Mobile-first menu site that loads fast on 3G/4G
  • Guided WhatsApp ordering: dishes, pick-up or delivery, time
  • MoMo payment link in chat, confirmed automatically
  • Clean, paid orders sent to a kitchen dashboard
  • Repeat-order shortcut for regular office customers

Designed to deliver: Fewer missed or mistyped orders, payments confirmed without screenshot checks, and staff freed up during the lunch rush.

Typical price: From $6,500 (site + ordering flow)Timeline: 4–5 weeks

Solution Real estate & diaspora buyers

Real-estate listing platform

The problem it solves: Developers and agents often get plenty of social media interest but few serious enquiries, while buyers abroad wait hours for a reply.

Key features

  • Listings with photos, floor plans and price in GHS or USD
  • Filters by location, bedrooms and budget
  • "Enquire on WhatsApp" with the listing details pre-filled
  • Instant first response and lead qualification across time zones
  • Weekly report: enquiries, viewings booked, cost per lead

Designed to deliver: Better-qualified enquiries, faster first replies to buyers in any time zone, and a clear weekly view of where leads come from.

Typical price: From $3,500 + Growth retainerTimeline: Leads live in 7 days; full site 4–5 weeks

Solution Private healthcare

Clinic WhatsApp assistant

The problem it solves: Clinic front desks are flooded with the same questions (hours, prices, insurance, directions), and evening messages wait until morning.

Key features

  • AI assistant trained on the clinic’s own FAQs and price list
  • Answers at any hour in plain, friendly language
  • Offers available appointment slots and takes the booking
  • Hands sensitive or medical questions straight to staff
  • Day-before reminders to reduce no-shows

Designed to deliver: Routine questions answered around the clock, bookings taken out of hours, fewer no-shows and a front desk with more time for patients.

Typical price: From $2,500Timeline: 2–3 weeks

Solution Professional services & B2B

Invoice automation

The problem it solves: Growing service businesses often chase late invoices by hand, and owners have no quick view of cash, sales and overdue accounts.

Key features

  • Polite WhatsApp and email reminders before and after due dates
  • MoMo and card payment links included in every reminder
  • Invoices marked paid automatically, receipts sent
  • Monday 8am summary to the owner’s WhatsApp
  • Documented workflows the team can see and adjust

Designed to deliver: Faster payment with less awkward chasing, no manual reconciliation of paid invoices, and a weekly cash snapshot in the owner’s pocket.

Typical price: From $2,000 (Quick-Win, up to 3 workflows)Timeline: 2–3 weeks
