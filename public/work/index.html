Restaurants & caterers

Menu, WhatsApp ordering and MoMo payment

The scenario: A busy Accra lunch spot takes most orders by WhatsApp. One staff member spends the rush copying orders onto paper and checking MoMo screenshots.

  • Mobile-first menu site that loads fast on 3G/4G
  • Guided WhatsApp ordering: dishes, pick-up or delivery, time
  • MoMo payment link in chat, confirmed automatically
  • Clean, paid orders sent to a kitchen dashboard
  • Repeat-order shortcut for regular office customers

Typical price: From GHS 14,000 (site + ordering flow)Timeline: 4–5 weeks

Invoice reminders and a weekly owner report
