Free Technology Assessment

See your whole technology setup on one page, and what to fix first.

A free, no-obligation review of how your business runs its computers, email, files and tools, across IT, marketing, automation and software. You get a written report in plain English with your top priorities, whether or not you work with us.

What we look at

Email & office tools

Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace: accounts, sharing and email delivery settings.

Devices & updates

What you have, how old it is and whether it is kept up to date.

Files & backup

Where important files live and what you would lose if a laptop died today.

Internet & Wi-Fi

How reliable your connection and office network are.

Staff setup & accounts

How long it takes to get a new person working, and to close accounts when someone leaves.

Software & subscriptions

What you pay for, who tracks renewals and what overlaps.

Website & online presence

How customers find you and what your website does for you.

Processes & automation

The repetitive tasks that take your team the most time.

How it works

  1. Short intake form

    About 5 to 7 minutes, below.

  2. Public checks

    Website status, domain renewal date and whether your email delivery settings are in place. We never ask for passwords.

  3. A 45-minute call

    With our founder, by video or phone, about how your business uses technology day to day.

  4. Written report

    Top priorities by area, quick wins you can do yourself and a 30/90/180-day roadmap.

  5. Optional review call

    20 minutes to go through the report and answer questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no obligation, and the report is yours to keep whether or not you work with us.

How long does it take?

The intake form takes about 5 to 7 minutes and the call about 45 minutes. Report turnaround is confirmed when we book your call.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. We only look at public information such as your website and domain records unless you invite us to look at more. We never ask for passwords; if you want us to see an admin screen, you share your screen.

Is this a security audit?

No. It is a point-in-time review of how your technology is set up and used, not a security audit or compliance review.
