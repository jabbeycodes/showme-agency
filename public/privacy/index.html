Privacy Policy

Privacy, plainly stated.

How ShowMe Digital Agency collects, uses and protects personal information. We never sell your data.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Who we are

This policy applies to ShowMe Digital Agency ("ShowMe", "we", "us") and to this website at agency.showmeworld.app. You can contact us about privacy at any time at info@showmeworld.app.

2. The information we collect

Information you give us

  • Consultation and contact requests: your name, company, email address, phone number, company size, areas of interest, current IT setup, timeline and message.
  • Conversations: emails, messages and notes from calls with you.
  • Client information: if you become a client, contact and billing details, materials you provide and the access we need to deliver the agreed services.

Information collected automatically

  • Anonymous usage counts: simple daily totals of page views and form submissions, recorded without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Form metadata: the time and your browser type, stored with your request to help prevent spam.
  • Hosting logs: our hosting provider processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website. One functional cookie remembers the region you choose on the site, if you choose one.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your request and arrange a consultation.
  • To scope, deliver, bill for and support the services you buy.
  • To keep our website working properly and understand overall usage.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We only send marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them, and every message explains how to opt out.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as website hosting, email and messaging providers, payment processors and the productivity tools we use to deliver your work, under appropriate confidentiality terms. We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Where information is processed

Our service providers and team members may process information in countries other than the one you live in. We choose reputable providers and take reasonable steps to protect information wherever it is processed.

6. How long we keep information

  • Requests that do not become engagements: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Client records: for the engagement and up to 6 years afterwards for accounting, tax and legal purposes.

7. How we protect information

We use access controls, strong authentication on our accounts, encrypted connections and reputable providers. Access is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfect; if an incident affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to receive a copy, and to ask us to correct or delete it. You may opt out of marketing at any time. To make a request, email info@showmeworld.app. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by applicable law, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. When we process data for clients

When we build or run systems for a client, the client controls their customers' data and we process it only on the client's instructions, keep it confidential and return or delete it when the engagement ends.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

12. Contact

Email info@showmeworld.app. See also our Terms of Service.
