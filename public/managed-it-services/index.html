Managed IT Services

Your Complete IT Department. Without the Cost of Building One.

A managed monthly service for your people, accounts, devices and systems: day-to-day support, proper administration, regular reporting and a plan for what comes next.

Book a Technology ConsultationCompare packages

The problem

IT that depends on whoever is least busy

In many growing organisations, IT is handled by a capable person who has another job. Accounts are created by hand, devices drift out of date, nobody is quite sure the backups work, and every new hire waits for equipment.

Hiring a full IT team is expensive. A managed service covers the routine work, with documentation and planning, for a predictable monthly fee.

What changes

  • One place for your team to get IT help
  • Accounts, licences and devices kept tidy and documented
  • Backups monitored and test-restored
  • New starters ready on day one; leavers removed properly
  • A roadmap and budget for upcoming changes

Not included

Clear boundaries keep the service predictable. These are quoted separately or passed through at cost.

  • Hardware, software and licence costs (passed through at cost or bought by you)
  • On-site visits, unless agreed as an add-on where available
  • Project work such as office moves, large migrations or new systems (quoted separately)
  • Systems that are no longer supported by their manufacturer
  • Faults in third-party services outside our control, which we escalate with the vendor on your behalf

Frequently asked questions

Who is this for?

Organisations without a full IT department, or with an IT lead who needs support. It suits teams that want day-to-day IT handled reliably and planned properly.

Do you replace our current IT provider?

Only if you want us to. We can take over fully, work alongside an internal IT person, or start with an assessment and a handover plan.

How is it priced?

As a monthly fee based on the package and the size of your team, quoted in writing after an assessment. Hardware, software and licences are separate.

Is there a minimum term?

Any minimum term is stated in your proposal before you sign.

What happens during onboarding?

We document your current setup, agree access and contacts, fix urgent issues and set up reporting before the steady monthly service begins.
