The problem

Stop being your own IT department

In many small and mid-sized businesses, the technology is spread across several people: whoever set up the email, a freelancer who built the website, a vendor for the network, and the owner filling the gaps at night. When something breaks, nobody owns it.

A managed plan makes one partner responsible for the routine work, with documentation, reporting and planning, for a predictable monthly fee.

Every plan includes

  • A written scope that says what is included and what is not
  • One point of contact who answers for the work
  • A regular plain-English report of what we did and what we recommend

Not included

Clear boundaries keep the service predictable.

  • Hardware, software licenses and third-party subscriptions (billed separately at cost or as quoted)
  • Projects such as migrations, office moves, new office setups or new systems (quoted separately)
  • On-site visits unless included in your plan (billable per visit or hour)
  • Support for personal devices, home networks or non-business software
  • Line-of-business software (for example practice-management or accounting systems) beyond basic access issues; we liaise with the vendor
  • Security assessments, testing, training or compliance work (not offered at present)
  • Out-of-hours monitoring or support unless separately agreed and confirmed

What we need from you

  • Name one internal contact
  • Give timely access and approvals
  • Keep hardware and licenses in support (we will advise)
  • Tell us about changes: new staff, new software, a new office
  • Do not share the admin access we use

Frequently asked questions

Who is this for?

Businesses with roughly 3 to 100 staff and no full-time IT person, or with an IT lead who needs support. It suits teams that want day-to-day IT handled properly and planned ahead.

How do we start?

With a Free Technology Assessment. We learn how your business uses technology today, give you a written summary of priorities, and only then recommend a plan, or tell you if you do not need one.

Do you replace our current IT provider?

Only if you want us to. We can take over fully, work alongside an internal IT person, or start with an assessment and a handover plan.

How is it priced?

As a monthly fee for the plan and the size of your team, plus a one-time onboarding fee, quoted in writing after the assessment. Hardware, software licenses and third-party tools are billed separately.

Is there a minimum term?

Any minimum term and notice period are stated in your proposal before you sign.

Is this a security service?

No. The plans cover IT support and administration, including routine settings such as backups, updates and multi-factor sign-in inside Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace. Security assessments, testing, training and compliance work are not offered at present.
