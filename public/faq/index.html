FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about how we work. If your question is not here, ask us.

Working with us

What does ShowMe Digital Agency do?

We are a technology and digital transformation partner. We provide managed IT services and infrastructure, digital marketing and brand growth, AI and automation, and software development and technology consulting.

How do engagements start?

With a technology consultation, followed by a short assessment and a written proposal. Nothing starts until you approve the proposal.

Do I have to buy everything from you?

No. Many clients start with one area. Because one team understands all four, it is easier to add more later.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. Accounts, domains, licences, content, brand assets and the code written for you are yours once paid for.

Managed IT

What is included in managed IT?

Day-to-day user support, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace administration, device inventory and updates, backup monitoring and regular reporting. Higher packages add onboarding and offboarding, network management, vendor coordination and planning.

What are your support hours and response times?

These are being finalised and will be set out in your service agreement. We only publish targets we can consistently meet.

Can you work with our existing IT person or provider?

Yes. We can support an internal IT lead, take over from a previous provider with a documented handover, or start with an assessment.

Projects and pricing

How is pricing set?

Projects get a fixed quote for the agreed scope; monthly services have a predictable monthly fee; advisory work is a fixed fee.

Which currency do you quote in?

Yes. Quotes are in US dollars (USD), and the exact figure is fixed in your proposal.

How can I pay?

Debit or credit card via a secure payment link, or bank transfer.

Do you guarantee rankings or a number of leads?

No honest agency can. We commit to the agreed work, transparency and clear reporting.

AI and automation

Will an AI assistant replace my staff?

No. It handles repetitive questions and routine steps, then hands off to a person for anything that needs judgement.

Where should we start with automation?

Usually with an automation review that identifies the workflows that waste the most time, so the first build pays off quickly.

Talk to us about your technology priorities.

Book a technology consultation. We listen first, then recommend the next practical step, whether or not it involves us.
