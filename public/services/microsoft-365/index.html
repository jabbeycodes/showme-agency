Managed IT Services & Infrastructure

Microsoft 365 & Business Email

Professional email on your domain, plus Microsoft 365 and SharePoint set up for your team.

Book a Technology Consultation Request pricing

The problem it solves

Running a business from a free personal email address looks unprofessional and scatters your files. Growing teams need proper email, shared files and collaboration.

What's included

  • Professional email on your own domain
  • Microsoft 365 licences and app setup
  • SharePoint and Teams configured for your team
  • Migration from your current email or files
  • User accounts, security basics and training

Who it's for

  • Businesses on free or personal email
  • Growing teams that need to collaborate
  • Companies wanting files organised and secure

What you can expect

  • A professional email address
  • Files and collaboration in one place
  • A team set up to work together

How we deliver it

  1. Assess

    We review your current email and files.

  2. Set up

    We configure Microsoft 365, email and SharePoint.

  3. Migrate

    We move your data with minimal disruption.

  4. Train

    We onboard your team and hand over admin.

Pricing

Setup for up to 5 users: domain email, Microsoft 365 apps, Teams and SharePoint basics, mailbox migration and a training session. Additional users can be added. Microsoft licence fees are paid separately to Microsoft.

Every engagement starts with a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% on launch, after you approve a working preview; monthly services are billed in advance. How pricing works.

Frequently asked questions

Do the Microsoft licences cost extra?

Yes. Our fee covers setup, migration and training; the monthly Microsoft licence fees are paid to Microsoft based on how many users you have.

Can you move my old emails?

Yes, migration from most common providers is part of the service.

Is my current work interrupted?

We plan migrations to minimise downtime, usually outside working hours where possible.
