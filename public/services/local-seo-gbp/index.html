Local SEO & Google Business Profile

Show up in the map pack and local searches when nearby customers are ready to buy.

Book a Technology Consultation Request pricing

The problem it solves

When someone nearby searches for what you sell, a competitor with a better-managed profile gets the call. An unclaimed or thin profile costs you walk-ins and inquiries.

What's included

  • Google Business Profile setup and optimization
  • Local keyword and competitor research for your area
  • Review generation strategy and response management
  • Consistent name, address and phone details across directories
  • Posts, photos and Q&A to keep your profile active
  • Monthly reporting on rankings, calls and directions

Who it's for

  • Businesses that serve a local area
  • Clinics, restaurants, salons and shops
  • Anyone relying on walk-ins and calls

What you can expect

  • More visibility in the local map pack
  • More calls, directions and walk-ins
  • A steady flow of fresh reviews

How we deliver it

  1. Audit

    We review your profile, reviews and local rankings.

  2. Optimize

    We complete and sharpen your profile and citations.

  3. Grow

    We build reviews and keep the profile active.

  4. Report

    You see rankings, calls and directions each month.

Pricing

Google Business Profile optimization, weekly posts, review-generation flow and responses, citation clean-up and a monthly ranking, calls and directions report. 3-month minimum.

Every engagement starts with a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% on launch, after you approve a working preview; monthly services are billed in advance. How pricing works.

Frequently asked questions

How long until I see results?

Local visibility usually improves over the first few months as the profile strengthens and reviews grow. We report progress monthly.

Can you get me more reviews?

Yes — we set up an ethical review-generation flow that makes it easy for happy customers to leave honest reviews.

Do you manage responses?

We can respond to reviews and questions on your behalf, in your voice, as part of the service.
