Managed IT Services & Infrastructure

Cloud Storage & Document Management

Get your files off scattered laptops and chat apps into an organized, shared system.

Book a Technology Consultation Request pricing

The problem it solves

Critical files live on one person’s laptop, in chat apps, or in inboxes. When that person is away or a device fails, work stops and documents disappear.

What's included

  • A cloud storage structure that fits how you work
  • Clear folders, naming and access permissions
  • Migration of existing files into the system
  • Backups so nothing is lost with a lost device
  • Team training on the new way of working

Who it's for

  • Teams sharing files over chat apps and email
  • Businesses worried about lost documents
  • Companies preparing to scale

What you can expect

  • One organized home for your files
  • Access control and backups
  • Less time hunting for documents

How we deliver it

  1. Review

    We map your current files and pain points.

  2. Design

    We design a folder and permission structure.

  3. Migrate

    We move files in and set up backups.

  4. Train

    We train the team on the new system.

Pricing

Folder and permission structure, migration of up to 50 GB of existing files, backup setup and one team training session.

Every engagement starts with a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% on launch, after you approve a working preview; monthly services are billed in advance. How pricing works.

Frequently asked questions

Which platform do you use?

Usually Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace — we recommend the best fit for your tools and budget.

Can you set permissions?

Yes. We control who can see and edit what, so sensitive files stay protected.

What about backups?

Backups are part of the setup so a lost or damaged device never means lost work.
