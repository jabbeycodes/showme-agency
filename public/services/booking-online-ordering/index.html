Booking & Online Ordering Systems

Let customers book appointments or place orders around the clock, without the back-and-forth.

The problem it solves

Booking by phone and DM means missed calls, double-bookings and no-shows. Your team spends the day coordinating instead of serving customers.

What's included

  • A booking or ordering flow customers can use any time
  • Availability, services and pricing you control
  • Automatic confirmations and reminders to cut no-shows
  • Optional deposits or payment via MoMo and card
  • A simple dashboard of upcoming bookings or orders

Who it's for

  • Clinics, salons and service providers
  • Restaurants and caterers taking orders
  • Any team losing time to manual scheduling

The outcomes

  • Fewer no-shows and double-bookings
  • Less time on the phone
  • Bookings and orders even after hours
