Business Websites

Fast, mobile-first websites that load on slow networks and turn visitors into enquiries.

Book a Technology Consultation Request pricing

The problem it solves

Most business websites are slow, hard to update, and treat the enquiry as an afterthought. Visitors bounce, and the ones who do reach out fall through the cracks.

What's included

  • A custom, mobile-first design built and tested from 375px up
  • Fast loading on any network (lazy-loaded images, lean code)
  • A content structure you can update yourself, with training and handover
  • Enquiry capture wired to WhatsApp and email so no lead goes cold
  • SEO foundations: clean titles, meta descriptions, schema and a sitemap
  • Google Analytics and Search Console set up at launch

Who it's for

  • Established businesses replacing a dated or DIY site
  • New companies that need to look credible from day one
  • Teams that want to update their own content without a developer

What you can expect

  • More enquiries from the same traffic
  • A site you actually control
  • A credible first impression on any device

How we deliver it

  1. Discover

    We map your customers, goals and the pages you need.

  2. Design

    You approve a real preview before we build a single page.

  3. Build

    We develop, wire up enquiry capture, and add SEO foundations.

  4. Launch

    We go live, train your team and hand over full access.

Pricing

Up to 6 custom pages, mobile-first design, enquiry capture to WhatsApp and email, SEO foundations, analytics, training and 30 days of launch support. Typical delivery 3–5 weeks.

Every engagement starts with a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% on launch, after you approve a working preview; monthly services are billed in advance. How pricing works.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

A typical business website takes 3–5 weeks from the signed proposal: about a week for discovery and design, two to three weeks to build, then review and launch. Quick content and approvals keep it at the shorter end.

Can I update it myself?

Yes. We build with content you can edit and train your team at handover, so you are never locked into a developer for small changes.

Do I own the website?

You own your domain, content and assets. We hand over full access at launch.
