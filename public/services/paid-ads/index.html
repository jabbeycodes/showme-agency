Digital Marketing & Brand Growth

Paid Ads (Google & Meta)

Managed Google and Meta campaigns aimed at qualified inquiries, not vanity clicks.

Book a Technology Consultation Request pricing

The problem it solves

Boosting posts and guessing at targeting burns budget. Without proper structure, tracking and follow-up, ad spend rarely turns into real customers.

What's included

  • Campaign strategy tied to a clear business goal
  • Google Search, Meta or both, based on where your buyers are
  • Audience, keyword and creative setup
  • Conversion tracking so results are measurable
  • Ongoing optimization and budget management
  • Transparent monthly reporting on cost per lead

Who it's for

  • Businesses that need inquiries now
  • Companies scaling an offer that already sells
  • Anyone tired of boosting with nothing to show

What you can expect

  • Lower cost per qualified lead over time
  • Spend focused on what works
  • Clear reporting you can trust

How we deliver it

  1. Plan

    We set the goal, audience and budget.

  2. Build

    We create campaigns, creative and tracking.

  3. Optimize

    We cut what fails and scale what works.

  4. Report

    You see spend, leads and cost per lead monthly.

Pricing

Management of Google or Meta campaigns (or both), creative, conversion tracking and monthly cost-per-lead reporting. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to the platforms; we recommend a starting budget in your quote.

Every engagement starts with a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% on launch, after you approve a working preview; monthly services are billed in advance. How pricing works.

Frequently asked questions

Is ad spend included in your fee?

No. Our fee covers management and reporting. Ad spend is separate and goes directly to Google or Meta.

What budget do I need?

It depends on your market and goal. We will advise on the right level for you.

Google or Meta?

Whichever reaches your buyers best — often both. We recommend the mix during planning.
