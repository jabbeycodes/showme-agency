Software Development & Technology Consulting

Integrations & SaaS Development

Connect the tools you use, or build and launch a software product of your own.

The problem it solves

Disconnected tools mean double entry and errors. And founders with a software idea often struggle to turn it into a real, sellable product.

What's included

  • Integrations so your tools share data automatically
  • APIs and connections between systems
  • A path from software idea to a launchable product
  • Payments, accounts and subscriptions for SaaS
  • Hosting, monitoring and ongoing iteration

Who it's for

  • Businesses with disconnected tools
  • Founders with a software product idea
  • Teams doing painful double entry

What you can expect

  • Data that flows automatically
  • Less manual re-entry and error
  • A product you can take to market

How we deliver it

  1. Discover

    We map the systems or the product idea.

  2. Design

    We plan the integrations or product scope.

  3. Build

    We develop and test in stages.

  4. Iterate

    We launch and improve with real usage.

Pricing

A single integration between two systems, built, tested and monitored. SaaS product builds start with a paid discovery sprint, credited toward the build.

Every engagement starts with a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% on launch, after you approve a working preview; monthly services are billed in advance. How pricing works.

Frequently asked questions

Can you integrate my payment and accounting tools?

In most cases, yes — we connect common payment, accounting and business tools so data flows without manual entry.

I have a SaaS idea — where do we start?

With discovery to define the smallest version worth launching, then a phased build so you learn from real users early.

Do you offer ongoing development?

Yes. Software is never truly finished; we can iterate as your needs and users grow.
