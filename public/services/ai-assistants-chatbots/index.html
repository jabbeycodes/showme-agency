AI Assistants & Chatbots

AI assistants for your website, messaging channels or internal tools that answer questions, qualify leads and hand off to a person.

Book a Technology Consultation Request pricing

The problem it solves

Customers and staff ask the same questions all day, across website chat, email and messaging apps. Slow answers lose sales, and your team drowns in repetitive work.

What's included

  • An AI assistant trained on your business, FAQs and documents
  • Instant replies to common questions, at any hour
  • Lead qualification that captures what you need
  • Smooth hand-off to a human when it matters
  • Deployment on your website, WhatsApp or internal tools
  • A knowledge source you can update as you grow

Who it's for

  • Businesses flooded with repetitive enquiries
  • Teams losing sales to slow replies
  • Operations teams answering the same internal questions

What you can expect

  • Instant replies, day and night
  • Qualified leads, not just messages
  • Time back for your team

How we deliver it

  1. Train

    We build a knowledge base from your FAQs and info.

  2. Design

    We design conversation and qualification flows.

  3. Connect

    We connect it to your website, WhatsApp or internal tools, with human hand-off.

  4. Improve

    We refine answers as real questions come in.

Pricing

Setup of an AI assistant trained on up to 50 FAQs, lead qualification and human hand-off, plus 30 days of tuning. Ongoing monthly care is available; WhatsApp and AI usage fees are passed through at cost.

Every engagement starts with a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% on launch, after you approve a working preview; monthly services are billed in advance. How pricing works.

Frequently asked questions

Will it replace my team?

No — it handles repetitive questions and qualifies leads, then hands off to a human for anything that needs a person.

Which channels can it run on?

Website chat, WhatsApp (through the official WhatsApp Business Platform where appropriate) and internal tools. We recommend the right setup for your volume and budget.

Can it take orders or bookings?

Yes. It can connect to your booking or ordering system so customers finish the request in the same conversation.
