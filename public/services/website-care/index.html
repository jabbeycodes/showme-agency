Website Care & Maintenance

Hosting, security, backups and updates so your site stays fast, safe and current.

Book a Technology Consultation Request pricing

The problem it solves

A website is not "done" at launch. Without updates and monitoring, sites break, slow down or get compromised — usually right when you need them.

What's included

  • Managed hosting and SSL certificate
  • Regular backups and security updates
  • Uptime and performance monitoring
  • A monthly allowance of small content changes
  • Priority support when something needs fixing

Who it's for

  • Businesses without an in-house web person
  • Owners who want peace of mind
  • Sites that need regular small updates

What you can expect

  • Less downtime and fewer surprises
  • A site that stays fast and secure
  • Someone accountable when things go wrong

How we deliver it

  1. Onboard

    We review your current setup and hosting.

  2. Secure

    We put backups, SSL and monitoring in place.

  3. Maintain

    We keep everything updated and handle small changes.

  4. Report

    You get a simple summary of what we did each month.

Pricing

Hosting, SSL, backups, security updates, uptime monitoring, up to 1 hour of small changes each month and a monthly summary. Month to month after a 3-month minimum.

Every engagement starts with a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% on launch, after you approve a working preview; monthly services are billed in advance. How pricing works.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to have built my site with you?

Not always — tell us what you are running and we will confirm whether we can take it on during a quick review.

What counts as a small change?

Text edits, swapping images, adding a page section and similar tasks. Larger work is quoted separately.

Can I cancel?

Care plans are month to month once any minimum term is met. You keep your site and assets.

Discuss this service

Tell us what you need and we will reply within one business day with next steps and a fixed quote.
