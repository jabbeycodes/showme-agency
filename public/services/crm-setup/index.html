CRM Setup

A simple CRM so every lead and customer is tracked and nothing slips through.

Book a Technology Consultation Request pricing

The problem it solves

Leads and customers tracked in heads, notebooks and chats get forgotten. Follow-ups slip, and you never see the full picture of your pipeline.

What's included

  • A CRM chosen and configured for your business
  • Lead capture connected to your website and WhatsApp
  • Pipeline stages that match how you sell
  • Follow-up reminders so leads are not forgotten
  • Team training and simple reporting

Who it's for

  • Sales teams losing track of leads
  • Businesses with slow or missed follow-up
  • Owners who want pipeline visibility

What you can expect

  • Every lead captured and tracked
  • Timely, consistent follow-up
  • A clear view of your pipeline

How we deliver it

  1. Map

    We map your sales process and stages.

  2. Configure

    We set up the CRM and lead capture.

  3. Connect

    We link your website, WhatsApp and email.

  4. Train

    We train your team and set up reporting.

Pricing

CRM selection and configuration, pipeline stages, website and WhatsApp lead capture, follow-up reminders and team training. CRM subscription fees are separate.

Every engagement starts with a fixed quote for the agreed scope. Projects are billed 50% to start and 50% on launch, after you approve a working preview; monthly services are billed in advance. How pricing works.

Frequently asked questions

Which CRM do you recommend?

We match the tool to your size and budget rather than forcing one platform — from lightweight options to full CRMs.

Can it capture WhatsApp leads?

Yes. We connect your website and WhatsApp so enquiries land in the CRM automatically where possible.

Will my team actually use it?

We keep it simple and train your team, because a CRM only works if people use it.
